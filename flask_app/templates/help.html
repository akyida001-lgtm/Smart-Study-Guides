Download formatted .docx with citations

1
Click "New Assignment" in the sidebar
Fill in your topic, subject area, desired word count, and citation style (APA, MLA, Harvard, or Chicago).
2
Source discovery
The system searches academic databases for real, verified sources. Review and approve sources before writing begins — you stay in control.
3
AI writing & humanization
The paper is written using your approved sources, then automatically rewritten to score 0% on AI detectors — all in one seamless step.
4
Download your Word document
Download as a properly formatted .docx — includes title page, in-text citations, and a full reference list.
💡 Tip: Be specific with your topic. Instead of "climate change", try "impact of deforestation on carbon emissions in Sub-Saharan Africa" — better sources, better paper.

Paste any AI-generated content

1
Open the Humanize tool
Click Humanize in the sidebar. Paste any AI-generated text — from ChatGPT, Gemini, or any writing tool.
2
Choose your tone
Select Standard (everyday academic), Formal (strict academic), or Advanced (sophisticated academic prose).
3
Run humanization
Click Humanize. The tool rewrites your text naturally while preserving the original meaning — bypassing AI detection models.
4
Copy or download
Copy the humanized output directly or download it as a Word document, ready to submit.
💡 Tip: Humanize in chunks of 500–800 words for best results, rather than pasting an entire 3,000-word essay at once.

Percentage score with highlighted sections

🟢 Safe zone

0–15% is safe for most institutions

1
Open AI Checker
Go to AI Checker in the sidebar. Paste your text or upload a .docx file to scan.
2
Run the scan
The checker uses the same detection models academic institutions rely on, returning a precise percentage AI score.
3
Read the results
Flagged sentences are highlighted. A score of 0–15% is generally safe. Anything above 30% should be humanized before submission.
4
Humanize flagged sections
Copy any flagged text into the Humanize tool, rewrite it, then re-run the checker until your score is in the safe zone.

CVs, cover letters, LinkedIn bios & more — 100% free service

▶️

🗂️ Document picker

Choose from 7 career document types

📋 Details form

Fill in your experience, skills & target role

⏳ Live generation

Watch the document write itself in real time

📥 Word & PDF export

Download as .docx or .pdf — ready to send

1
Open Career Section — it's completely free
No credits needed. Click Career Section in the sidebar. All registered users get access at no cost.
2
Choose your document type
Seven professional documents are available:
  • 📄 CV — Full curriculum vitae (academic & work history)
  • ✉️ Cover Letter — Tailored to a specific job posting
  • 🔗 LinkedIn Bio — Professional summary for your profile
  • 🎓 Personal Statement — For university or postgrad applications
  • 📜 Reference Letter — Written on behalf of a colleague or student
  • 🎤 Interview Prep — Q&A practice for a specific role
  • 📋 Job Description — For employers posting a vacancy
3
Fill in your details
Each document type has its own form. Enter your work history, target job title, skills, education, and any specific instructions. The more detail you provide, the more personalised the output.
4
Generate & watch it write in real time
Click Generate. You'll see the document writing itself live on screen — a streaming view of the content as it's created.
5
Build & download Word or PDF
Once complete, click 📄 Build Document. This generates a professionally formatted file in your chosen industry style. Download as Word (.docx) or PDF — both are ready to send to employers.
🎨 Industry-specific styles
Your document is formatted to match professional standards for your industry:
🏢 Corporate 🎨 Creative 💻 Technical 🏨 Hospitality 🏥 Medical 🎓 Education 📋 General
💡 Access: The Career Section is free for all subscribers and for anyone who has referred a user who subscribes. No monthly limits apply — generate as many documents as you need.

Viewing billing history
