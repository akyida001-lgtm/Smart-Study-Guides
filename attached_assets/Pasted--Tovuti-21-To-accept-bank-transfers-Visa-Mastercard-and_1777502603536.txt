Yes, you can accept Google Pay, Apple Pay, and certain mobile money transfers, but how they work depends on your customer's location and your integration. 
1. Google Pay and Apple Pay
These are supported automatically through PayPal Checkout (Smart Buttons).
Availability: When a customer uses a device with Google Pay or Apple Pay enabled, the smart buttons will detect it and display the corresponding button.
Requirement: You must use the latest PayPal JavaScript SDK (the standard "Smart Button" code) for these to appear dynamically to your customers.

2. Mobile Money (M-Pesa and Airtel Money)
PayPal does not natively include M-Pesa or Airtel Money as a direct button in its standard global gateway. However, you can manage this in two ways:
Google Pay Integration (Kenya): In Kenya, users can link Airtel Money or M-Pesa directly to their Google Pay/Google Play accounts. If your customer has done this, they can pay you via the Google Pay button on your PayPal gateway.
PayPal-to-M-Pesa Service: Specifically for Kenyan merchants, you can use the PayPal-Mobile Money service with M-PESA to move money between your PayPal business account and your M-Pesa wallet once you've received payment.
