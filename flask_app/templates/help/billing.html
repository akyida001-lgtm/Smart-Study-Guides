How the Weekly Pass works
Pay $25 once for 7 days of access — up to 3 short assignments per day. No auto-renewal. If you buy another pass while one is active, the new week starts when the current one ends.
2
What it covers & discounts
The pass is for short work: essays, discussion posts, presentations and everyday assignments. Theses, dissertations and other long projects are not supported. Your first pass gets an automatic 10% new-subscriber discount. Referral users also get 10% off.
3
Accepted payment methods
💳 Bank cards — Visa, Mastercard, Amex from any country
🅖 Google Pay — on Chrome / Android
🍎 Apple Pay — on Safari / iPhone
4
Viewing billing history
All payments are logged under My Subscription in the sidebar. You can see your plan status, expiry date, and past payments.
💡 Tip: Upload your assignment, buy the pass, and finish everything you need within the 7 days — the AI Checker is unlimited while it is active.
