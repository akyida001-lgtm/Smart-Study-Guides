Humanity is now faced with one of the most significant issues: global warming. Furthermore, the consequences of global warming reach far beyond that of the ecosystem; global warming will significantly affect the economy. As the Earth's average air temperature rises due to the increase of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere, we see the implications of climate change increase in frequency and severity, including extreme weather events, rising sea levels, and loss of habitats. As a result, addressing the challenges of global warming requires immediate and urgent action: technological innovation, policy reform, and international cooperation are all critical factors in successfully combating global warming. 

**Impacts of Global Warming**  
The rise in temperature due to global warming has led to tremendous disruption of natural ecosystems. Global warming has contributed to the melting of the polar ice caps, which jeopardizes the survival of various species that depend on these habitat areas (Day, 2019). For example, penguins residing in Antarctica are losing their habitat and food sources due to the shortage of ice (Day, 2019). Since ecosystems are interconnected, the decline of one species can have an impact on all other species throughout the food chain, which makes understanding these impacts essential towards effective environmental management.

**Potential Mitigation Strategies**  
A multi-pronged approach that relies on both technology and policy development is essential to mitigate the effects of global warming. According to Bockris (2010), there is an immediate need to implement new and innovative solutions, including renewable forms of energy and enhancing energy efficiency, to reduce the amount of greenhouse gases emitted into the atmosphere. Lastly, further implementing policies that promote sustainable behaviors and practices can reduce the effects of climate change, e.g., implementing a carbon price and providing subsidies to businesses and individuals for adopting green technologies (Bockris, 2010). Consequently, the combination of these methods can create a much more sustainable world. 

This paper has illustrated that global warming represents a threat to both the environment and overall biodiversity; the devastation upon ecosystems, as experienced in Antarctica, is of increasing concern and urgency and must be addressed. With an emphasis on implementing new technology through innovation and reforming environmental policies; we must work to overcome the obstacles presented by climate change and preserve the planet for our children's grandchildren.
